An in-memory regression fixture supplied eight pairs with balanced but different paper panels between seeds. The old reporter accepted the endpoint completion flags, checked agreement only inside each pair, and issued a superiority verdict without a freeze. There were no real outcomes in this demonstration. The corrected scorer and reporter check the same frozen source, checkpoint, registered budget and paper population. Each public row commits to its ID, manuscript hash and historical label. Correctness is recomputed from the decision; a malformed decision scores zero. The full panel must reproduce the registered ordered fingerprint and class balance. The primary analysis pairs search seeds and shares each resampled paper column across both arms and all seeds. Resampling is independent between the seed dimension and the two within-class paper samples. A positive lower bound supports local superiority; an upper bound at or below zero is the registered evidence-against rule. An interval spanning positive effects and zero is inconclusive. An all-tie sample gives a degenerate interval under this rule. It establishes observed ties and cannot establish population equivalence. With a valid joint freeze, completeness is judged separately for each comparison. The sensitivity endpoints use the last checkpoint under a common logical-token ceiling, so realized costs may differ.
