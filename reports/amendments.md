# Prospective amendments

## 20 November 2026 · Manuscript cleaning before any model call

An audit of the registered training and validation inputs found publication mastheads joined to scientific text or a page number by PDF extraction. Some titles also contained the literal double-blind review masthead. The original cleaner missed those cases. We removed the boundary assumption, stripped the blind-review masthead and handled whitespace in OpenReview URL prefixes. We preserved surrounding manuscript text and phrases such as “final decision” when they describe a scientific task. The source CSV, grouping rules, sampling seed, methods and statistical decision rule retain their registered definitions. The current split file fingerprints the corrected text. The original Git commit preserves the previous fingerprints. The audit used no model predictions or sealed test outcomes. ## 26 November 2026 · Development-text audit and endpoint integrity

The independent review found a strong association between acknowledgments headings and historical decisions on training and validation manuscripts. These two splits informed this repair. The study has a logged synthetic hardware request and no recorded search or endpoint predictions at amendment time. The intervention below was specified before mechanical processing of pilot and test text. The original November registration remains the historical record. [Previous cohort](../results/split-manifest.2026-11-20.json) · [Registered decision rule](preregistration.md)

Keep the ordered IDs and labels of every registered split. Reproduce the November 20 text fingerprints from the released CSV before applying a common, label-independent text transform to the enrolled manuscripts. Strip identifiable acknowledgment and funding-disclosure headings, their prose, and identifiable funding or reviewer-thanks statements while preserving adjacent scientific sections, references, and ordinary scientific uses of these words. Use training and validation only to design and inspect the transform. Fix its source before processing either held-out split. Report cue counts and character-length distributions by historical class on development only. Treat provenance of manuscript versions and residual publication cues as limitations on the historical-decision estimand. [Leakage and target validity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.07048)

For the primary comparison the eligible cohort comprises the eight registered editable/native seed pairs. A valid joint freeze and complete scoring on the same registered 100 papers are required. Missing secondary controls or the single midpoint continuation are reported separately. An incomplete primary comparison is descriptive. Invalid data cannot receive a verdict. The token-budget comparison is a secondary sensitivity analysis. The primary metric, interval algorithm, seed, thresholds, and search budget rule retain their registered definitions.

The first frozen transform removed the detected headings. Inspection of residual funding-language matches in development text found a credit block formatted as a table caption following the bibliography. The second transform recognizes the end of the bibliography at a figure/table record and removes a credit-only caption while retaining scientific captions. The first-pass implementation and fingerprints are retained in Git. Neither held-out text nor held-out cue counts informed the correction.
