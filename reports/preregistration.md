## Hypothesis and unit of comparison

At the same validation budget, a newest-agent executable parent policy evolved from uniform selection produces a reviewer with higher historical-decision accuracy than clade Thompson sampling. The primary experimental unit is an independent paired search seed. Within each pair, score both frozen reviewers on the same sealed 100 manuscripts. The estimate is the mean of the per-seed accuracy differences, in percentage points.

## Inputs and eligibility

Use the pinned HyperAgents CSV. Decode the manuscript representation and remove decision/header metadata, split by duplicate-connected manuscript groups, discard conflicting group labels, and select one representative per group. Hash-order seed 42 produces 100 train, 100 validation, 100 test and 24 further pilot-only papers, balanced by historical outcome. The producer program and actual split hashes are committed with this registration. The writer sees the title and abstract. The reviewer sees the full cleaned paper if it fits. Otherwise a single frozen, deterministic reader processes every contiguous chunk, and the final reviewer receives its notes. Record coverage, route, chunk count and input/output tokens. The trusted scorer alone opens labels. Training feedback uses training papers only. Confirmation freezes every endpoint and the selected search budget before opening test inputs. Pilot reporting uses only the pilot set.

## Search controls and estimand

Every workspace owns writer, reviewer, meta-agent and selector programs. The most recent valid node supplies the selector, the chosen parent supplies the program to edit, and its valid child becomes the next selector owner. The root is uniform. Recover runtime selector failures using earlier valid owners and finally the uniform root. Log the full fallback chain. Fixed arms use native clade Thompson, uniform, and the released HyperAgents sigmoid times exponential child penalty. Supply its score from the mean of current per-role Beta posterior means, including the neutral Beta(1,1) prior for unmeasured roles. Diagnostic replay uses the paper's reciprocal penalty on the same snapshot with the same uniform variate.

Follow October Algorithm 1 with expansion exponent 0.6, budget-remaining Thompson cooldown, evaluation from own counts, and least-measured eligible role/task scheduling. Collect three training examples on first expanding a parent. The reviewer predicts historical accept/reject decisions. The writer is scored by the epoch's frozen reviewer. Preserve human records and manuscripts while erasing displaced-reviewer-dependent outcomes. Use checkpoints at 8, 16, 32 and subsequent powers of two through the budget. The optional adversarial objective is off in all arms. At checkpoint elections, score each distinct reviewer program on the same eight validation anchor papers (four of each class), cache by program and paper, and count distinct paper identities. This small, fixed packet is a local compute choice shared by every arm. Select the final reviewer specialist using all distinct human validation decisions available for its program, including the packet. Use the 0.05 Beta quantile, at least five distinct observations, retain the incumbent on election ties, and use earliest admission to break endpoint ties. A malformed task decision counts as a failure. Model service outages are recoverable infrastructure interruptions.

## Stopping and analysis

Pilot seed 0 has 64 validation steps per arm. Prespecify confirmatory paired seeds 1 through 8 and controls on seeds 1 through 5. Using pilot throughput only, choose the largest of 64, 128 and 256 outcomes per search fitting the remaining week including sealed scoring, the ablation continuation and all planned arms. If even 64 is projected to overrun, keep 64 and prioritize the primary pairs. Use the worst completed pilot-arm seconds per search evaluation and worst observed held-out seconds per item, accounting separately for post-search evaluation. The first-14-hour target permits documented engineering repairs. Count all attempted runs. Retain original failed pilot versions. A change to scientific settings after unblinding requires an amendment and a fresh confirmatory cohort.

At the midpoint of the first editable confirmatory seed fork its exact archive, evaluator and RNG states. Continue its normal trajectory and override only future parent choices with native Thompson in the counterfactual. Evaluate both frozen endpoints after the manifest is locked. The fork is a single causal case study. Score the unevolved root as a fixed reference. Also freeze reviewer identities at checkpoints for an equal-token sensitivity analysis, using the last checkpoint at or below the lower total logical search-token usage in each seed pair. Count input and output tokens separately and sum them for the token-matched diagnostic.

Use 10,000 paired crossed bootstrap draws (analysis seed 1701). Resample seeds with replacement; independently resample shared paper IDs within decision class with replacement; retain the pairing across arms. Report the two-sided percentile 95% interval, individual paired effects, missing pairs, test coverage by reader route, and actual compute. Lower bound above zero supports local superiority; upper bound at or below zero is evidence against it; otherwise inconclusive. An incomplete cohort is descriptive. Development outcomes, arm-level uncertainty, learned-policy complexity and replay disagreement are secondary. The analysis does not measure intrinsic scientific quality or exclude pretraining familiarity.
